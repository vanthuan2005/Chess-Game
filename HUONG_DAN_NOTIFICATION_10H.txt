THEM VAO AndroidManifest.xml

1) Them permission nay TRUC TIEP ben trong <manifest ...>, nam ngoai <application>:

<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS" />

2) Them receiver nay BEN TRONG <application ...>:

<receiver
    android:name=".DailyNotificationReceiver"
    android:enabled="true"
    android:exported="false" />

3) Thay SplashActivity.java hien tai bang file SplashActivity.java moi.

4) Copy 2 file moi vao:
app/src/main/java/com/example/chessgame/

- DailyNotificationManager.java
- DailyNotificationReceiver.java

CACH HOAT DONG
- Lan dau mo app, Android 13+ se hoi quyen gui thong bao.
- App hen thong bao hang ngay luc 10:00.
- Neu luc dang ky da qua 10:00, lan thong bao dau tien se la 10:00 ngay hom sau.
- Bam vao notification se mo Chess Game.
- AlarmManager setInexactRepeating co the bi Android dich vai phut do toi uu pin.
